## The problem
Small clinics lose bookings when nobody picks up the phone, and no-shows cost them time and money.

## What I did
Through my agency, Your Space, Virtually, I built voice AI agents for clinic clients:

- It answers the call and books the appointment end to end.
- It adds the booking straight to the clinic's calendar.
- It asks the patient when they'd like to be reminded, then calls them back at exactly that time.

I work with clients directly across countries and time zones, from scoping what they need to delivering a working system.
